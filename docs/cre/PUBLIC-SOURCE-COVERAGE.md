# CRE public-source coverage, 23 September 2026

The new `market`, `development`, and `local-news` dossier sections use fixed, keyless public sources. The selected point is used to identify a reporting geography or nearby municipal records. None of these sections verifies a property's rent roll, occupancy, value, construction status, or code compliance.

The public `Code Violations` datasets found on Dallas Open Data have a last row-update timestamp in **February 2019**, despite newer search crawl dates and automated-update metadata. They do not support a current parcel conclusion. The section therefore marks code cases **not checked** and links to the [DallasNow official case portal](https://aca-prod.accela.com/dallastx/default.aspx) or the [Atlanta Accela official portal](https://aca-prod.accela.com/ATLANTA_GA/Default.aspx). A service request or complaint must not be reported as an adjudicated violation. No current code-case record was verified for either pilot point.

`refresh=1` on the new routes and the point-scoped rents, amenities, destinations, schools, and site routes bypasses eligible fresh entries at most once per cache key every **30 seconds**. Concurrent requests share an in-flight load. A failed refresh can preserve a dated last-good result and reports an attempted time separately from the last successful retrieval. Normal TTLs are 7 days for Zillow market files and amenities, 1 day for municipal development and named destinations, 6 hours for city-topic news, and 30 days for NCES schools. Larger existing national datasets and provider-specific archives retain their established cache windows; Update must not be interpreted as a guaranteed re-download of every underlying file. Source observation dates can predate retrieval by months or years.

The added workplace query is implemented and covered by fixture tests. A later live Royal Lane-area read-back on 23 September **did** return six named OSM workplace candidates, with `osm_base` at **20:21:04 UTC**: Allstate (1,797 m), Blue Nova Logistics (2,043 m), Precision Global Corporation (2,074 m), Michele Davis-State Farm (2,533 m), Texas Health Presbyterian Hospital Dallas (2,593 m), and First Baptist Medical Center (2,625 m). The same public mirrors had refused or timed out in earlier attempts, so the route retains its 6 MB response cap, 5 km radius, one-day cache and stale-last-good policy. A future failed source request must show unavailable or dated cached coverage rather than inventing employer labels. These names and distances are OSM map observations, not verified staffing or major-employer claims.

All external hosts and query shapes are fixed in server modules. No user-provided URL is fetched. News headline text is treated as untrusted data, limited in length, and rendered as text with links restricted to the Google News article host. Zillow attribution and the municipal/news publisher terms must be reviewed before redistributing bulk source data in a public hosted service.
